# ATS Resume, Cover Letter & Job Application Guide

A practical collection of resources to help create **ATS-friendly resumes, Harvard-style resumes, tailored cover letters, and professional resume emails** for Data Analyst and other job applications.

## 📁 What This Repository Covers

### 1. ATS-Friendly Resume

An ATS (Applicant Tracking System) resume is designed to be easy for both recruiters and resume-screening software to read.

**Key guidelines:**
- Use a simple, clean layout.
- Use standard section headings such as `Summary`, `Skills`, `Experience`, `Projects`, and `Education`.
- Include relevant keywords from the job description.
- Focus on measurable achievements rather than only responsibilities.
- Use clear job titles, dates, and locations.
- Avoid tables, text boxes, excessive graphics, icons, and complex formatting.
- Save the final resume as a PDF unless the job posting specifies another format.

**Recommended sections:**
1. Professional Summary
2. Technical Skills
3. Professional Experience
4. Projects
5. Education
6. Certifications
7. Additional Information

## 2. Harvard-Style Resume

The Harvard-style resume approach focuses on **clarity, concise content, strong action verbs, and achievement-oriented bullet points**.

### Example

Instead of:

> Responsible for creating sales reports in Excel.

Write:

> Created automated Excel sales reports using PivotTables and VLOOKUP, reducing daily reporting effort by 2 hours.

A strong bullet generally follows:

**Action + Task + Tool/Method + Measurable Result**

### Example Structure

**DATA ANALYST | Company Name | Location | Jan 2024 – Jun 2025**

- Analyzed operational data using **SQL, Python, and Excel** to identify trends and support business decisions.
- Built interactive **Power BI dashboards** to monitor KPIs and improve reporting visibility.
- Automated recurring reporting workflows, reducing manual effort and improving reporting efficiency.

## 3. Cover Letter

A good cover letter should be **short, specific, and tailored to the company and role**.

### Recommended Structure

**Subject:** Application for Data Analyst – [Your Name]

**Dear Hiring Manager,**

I am writing to apply for the **[Job Title]** position at **[Company Name]**. With experience in **SQL, Python, Power BI, and Excel**, I have worked on data analysis, dashboard development, reporting, and business insights.

In my previous/project work, I have analyzed datasets, created interactive dashboards, automated reporting tasks, and translated data into actionable insights. I am particularly interested in this opportunity because **[specific reason related to the company or role]**.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my skills and experience can contribute to your team.

**Best regards,**  
[Your Name]  
[Phone Number]  
[Email]  
[LinkedIn]  
[Portfolio/GitHub]

### Cover Letter Tips

- Keep it to one page.
- Customize it for each job.
- Mention the exact job title.
- Connect your skills to the job requirements.
- Avoid repeating your entire resume.
- Use specific achievements where possible.
- Keep the language professional and natural.

## 🔍 ATS Resume Checklist

Before submitting your resume:

- [ ] Resume is tailored to the job description
- [ ] Relevant keywords are included naturally
- [ ] Job title matches the target role where appropriate
- [ ] Skills are easy to find
- [ ] Experience bullets show measurable impact
- [ ] Dates and job titles are consistent
- [ ] No spelling or grammar errors
- [ ] Contact details are correct
- [ ] LinkedIn and portfolio links work
- [ ] Resume is easy to read
- [ ] File name is professional
- [ ] PDF opens correctly
- [ ] Cover letter is customized when required
